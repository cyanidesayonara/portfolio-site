# Sound effect sources (3D walk)

Every clip here is CC0 or public domain, so no credit is required; this list records where each one came from. Clips were trimmed, high-passed at 85 Hz, lightly denoised (`afftdn`), peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, given short fades and encoded as 96 kbps mono MP3 with ffmpeg.
