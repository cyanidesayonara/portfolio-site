An interactive mobile game designed for cats. Built with Flutter, it has a set of moving toys that cats can chase and "catch" on the screen, each with its own behavior and feedback.

## Toys

- A laser dot that darts around unpredictably
- A mouse that scurries and hides
- A feather that floats and drifts
- A bug that can be squished
- A ball of yarn that bounces realistically

Each toy has its own reaction when caught, with sound effects and haptic feedback, in a full-screen, touch-optimized (paw-optimized) interface for iOS and Android.
