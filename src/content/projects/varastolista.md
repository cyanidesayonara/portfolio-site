A streamlined inventory management application built for the Hospital District of Helsinki's warehouse department, to track thousands of critical spare parts. It replaced paper records with a searchable database, so technicians can find a part quickly during an urgent repair.

- Create, search and manage spare parts
- QR code scanning for quick part number input
- Excel import and export
